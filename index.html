A few honest answers

Things you might be wondering

Is NEXORA really free to use?

Yes. The Free plan includes every core learning and productivity tool. Plus adds aesthetic and personalisation extras. This static demo does not process payments at all.

Is the AI actually connected?

Not yet. The interface is a clearly-labelled demo that responds with prepared study guidance. Real AI would connect through a secure backend so no private key ever lives in the browser.

Where is my work stored?

Your notes, tasks, progress and theme choices are stored in this browser’s local storage. That’s convenient but browser-specific — it is not a secure cloud backup.

What does “demo Plus” mean?

It lets you try every Plus design feature locally. No card, payment, subscription or account is involved. You can switch back to Free whenever you like in Pricing.

Does Scan & Solve really read my photo?

It previews and validates your image locally, but real OCR and question solving require an external backend. For now, type or edit the question and you’ll see a demo explanation.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. NEXORA is responsive, keyboard friendly and respects reduced-motion preferences. It works in modern browsers and needs no build step or installation.

The honest small print

Will I be charged?

No. There is no checkout, card field or payment integration. Plus is a local demo toggle for this static project. Real billing needs a provider such as Stripe and secure server-side verification.

Can I go back to Free?

Yes. Use “Switch to Free” on this page or in Settings › Membership. Your notes, courses and tasks remain exactly where they are; only Plus design options become locked.

Where do my themes and progress live?

In this browser’s local storage. They persist between visits on the same device but do not sync or act as a secure backup. Cross-device sync is planned for a future database-backed version.

Is NEXORA affiliated with YouTube?

No. Video Studio embeds public YouTube links using the standard privacy-enhanced player. NEXORA does not host, re-upload or claim ownership of those videos.
